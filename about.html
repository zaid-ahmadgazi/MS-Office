About Deskwise

Deskwise exists to answer one kind of question well: "how do I do [specific thing] in [Office app]?"

What we publish

Every guide starts from a real, specific task — not a general overview of an app. We'd rather publish one clear answer to "how do I remove a blank page in Word" than a broad "Word tutorial" that covers everything shallowly and nothing well.

Editorial approach

Steps are written and checked against the current version of the relevant Office app at the time of publishing. When Microsoft changes a menu or a ribbon layout, guides are re-checked and the "updated" date on the article reflects that. If a guide hasn't been re-verified after an interface change, we'd rather you know that than assume it's current.

Independence

Deskwise is an independent educational resource. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Microsoft. Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Access, Outlook and Microsoft 365 are trademarks of the Microsoft group of companies; we use those names only to describe the software our tutorials cover.

Who writes these guides

Content is produced and edited by the Deskwise editorial team. We haven't invented awards, certifications, or user counts to put here — if that information becomes real and verifiable, it will be added.

Get in touch if you've spotted an error or an interface change we haven't caught yet.
