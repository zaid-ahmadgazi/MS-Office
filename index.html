How to Remove a Blank Page in Word

Deskwise Editorial Team Updated Jul 14, 2026 6 min read
Short answer

Turn on formatting marks (Ctrl+Shift+8) to see what's actually on the blank page, then remove the specific cause: an extra paragraph mark, a manual page break, a trailing table, or a section break. Deleting text alone usually doesn't fix it.

A blank page at the end of a Word document is almost never "empty" — something on it is holding the page open. Here are the four causes, checked in the order they're most likely to apply.

1. Extra paragraph marks

The most common cause: several empty paragraphs stacked up at the end of the document.

  1. Show formatting marks

    Press Ctrl+Shift+8 (or ⌘+8 on Mac) to reveal paragraph marks (¶).

  2. Select the extra marks

    Click at the end of your real content, then select down to the end of the document.

  3. Delete

    Press Delete. The blank page should disappear immediately.

Screenshot: formatting marks showing several empty ¶ symbols at the end of a document

2. A manual page break

If you see a single "Page Break" label rather than a stack of ¶ marks, someone (possibly you, a while ago) inserted a manual page break. Click right before it and press Delete once.

Tip

If deleting once doesn't work, place your cursor immediately after the break and press Backspace instead — this handles cases where the break is at the very start of what would otherwise be the last line.

3. A table that ends on the last row of a page

Word always keeps a paragraph mark after a table, and if the table itself fills the page exactly, that trailing paragraph gets pushed onto a new page. You can't delete that paragraph mark, but you can shrink its font size to close to zero (e.g. 1pt) so it no longer forces a new page.

Common mistake

Trying to select and delete the paragraph mark after a table. Word won't let you remove it — the fix is to shrink it, not delete it.

4. A section break with different page setup

If the blank page appeared right after you changed page orientation or margins partway through the document, a section break is the cause. Turn on formatting marks, find the "Section Break" label, and delete it if you no longer need two different layouts.

FAQ

Why won't Backspace delete my blank page?

Backspace only removes what your cursor is next to. If the page is caused by a section break or table property rather than a paragraph mark, you need to remove or change that element specifically, not just delete text.

Is it safe to turn on formatting marks while I do this?

Yes — showing formatting marks (Ctrl+Shift+8) only changes what you see on screen. It doesn't change the document, and it makes the actual cause of the blank page visible.
